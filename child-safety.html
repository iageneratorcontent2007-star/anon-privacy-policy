Anon Child Safety Standards

Anon does not allow child sexual abuse and exploitation in any form. We prohibit content, behavior, and attempts to use the app to harm, sexualize, exploit, or endanger minors.

Zero tolerance: Anon strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), sexual content involving minors, sexual exploitation or grooming of minors, and any attempt to establish sexual contact with a minor.

Prohibited Content and Conduct

  • Any child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including uploads, links, requests, or distribution.
  • Any sexual content involving minors, whether real, solicited, described, promoted, or shared.
  • Sexual exploitation of minors, including coercion, manipulation, extortion, or abuse.
  • Grooming behavior or any attempt to build contact with a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Attempts to arrange, encourage, or initiate sexual contact with a minor on or off the platform.
  • Using Anon to facilitate illegal sexual activity involving minors in any jurisdiction.

Reporting in the App

Users can report violations directly in the Anon app. Reports help us review suspected abuse, dangerous behavior, or other content that may violate these standards.

We review complaints and, when appropriate, take action based on the information available to us and our applicable legal obligations.

Enforcement

When we determine that content or conduct violates these standards, we may take measures such as:

  • Removing prohibited content.
  • Blocking or restricting accounts involved in the violation.
  • Disabling access to features where necessary to protect users.
  • Preserving relevant information when required for safety review or legal compliance.

Cooperation With Law Enforcement

Where required or appropriate under applicable law, Anon may cooperate with law enforcement or other competent authorities in relation to child safety, child sexual abuse and exploitation, or other unlawful conduct involving minors.
